Description

>

'Whatever People Say I Am, That's What I'm Not' is Arctic Monkeys' debut studio album. It was released in the United Kingdom by Domino Recording Company and in the United States by Domino and Rough Trade Records. The album was produced by Jim Abbiss, who had previously worked with the band on their debut single "I Bet You Look Good on the Dancefloor" and B-sides. The album and its songs are a representation of the social working-class culture in Sheffield at the time.
