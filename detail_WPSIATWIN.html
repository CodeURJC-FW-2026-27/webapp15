Whatever People Say I Am, That's What I'm Not is the debut studio album by English rock band Arctic Monkeys. It was released on 23 January 2006 in the United Kingdom by Domino Recording Company and in the United States by Domino and Rough Trade Records. The album was produced by Jim Abbiss, who had previously worked with the band on their debut single "I Bet You Look Good on the Dancefloor" and B-sides.
